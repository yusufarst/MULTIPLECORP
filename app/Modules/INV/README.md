# Inventory (INV)

Tier 5 of [ARCHITECTURE §3](../../../docs/04-architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md#3-modules). `Models/` holds the Eloquent models of its tables (TASK-008) but those keyed by several columns, which an Eloquent key cannot be — `stock_scope_balances`, `stock_lot_balances`, `stock_reversal_balances`, `stock_count_scopes`, `unattributed_loss_exposures` and `unattributed_loss_group_exposures`, each written by one statement of its posting path (DATABASE §1) —; `stock_product_balances`' and `stock_consumption_balances`' have no factory, a balance belonging to its posting service (ARCHITECTURE §7); its other Tasks add the rest.

Tables ([DATABASE §4.7](../../../docs/04-architecture/DATABASE/s04-07-inv.md), 26): `locations`, `inventory_lots`, `serial_units`, `stock_movements`, `stock_movement_entries`, `receiving_lines`, `dispatch_lines`, `stock_consumptions`, `stock_consumption_balances`, `stock_restorations`, `stock_product_balances`, `stock_scope_balances`, `stock_lot_balances`, `stock_reversal_balances`, `reservations`, `reservation_events`, `stock_counts`, `stock_count_scopes`, `stock_count_lines`, `stock_count_findings`, `unattributed_loss_cases`, `unattributed_loss_candidates`, `unattributed_loss_exposures`, `unattributed_loss_group_exposures`, `unattributed_loss_resolutions`, `unattributed_loss_resolution_lines`.

Receiving (TASK-039): `Actions/ReceiveGoods` and `Actions/ReverseReceipt` over `Services/ReceivingRecords` (AX-04; AX-05), the ledger through `InventoryPosting::ledger`; `Services/ReceivingSubjects` supplies DOC-12; `Queries/ReceivingQueries` the receiving projection of PG-021 and PG-022, never a price, tax, charge or cost (PJ-18); `Queries/ScanLookup` the exact lookup of ROUTE-282 under the scanner's own limiter (QB-06; WS-12).

Stock views and reversal (TASK-040): `Queries/StockViews` and `Queries/MovementHistory` are physical only and never select a cost table (PJ-01); cost by family comes from CST's `CostFigures`. `Actions/ReverseMovement` over `Services/MovementReversal` is ROUTE-212 (NX-11): a MOVEMENT_REVERSAL capped by the reversal guard or the case's counters, or capped restorations for a movement that created consumptions.
