OWNER DESIGN DIRECTIVE — GOLDEN VISUAL FIDELITY AND REFINEMENT

I am clarifying my definitive visual expectations for MULTIPLECORP following the GF-1 pilot rejection.

MY PRIMARY DESIGN REQUIREMENT

I want MULTIPLECORP to reproduce the original Golden frontend as faithfully as technically possible.

Golden is the authoritative reference for:

- Visual design and overall aesthetic.
- Layout, composition, and component proportions.
- Typography, colors, surfaces, shadows, borders, and icons.
- Navigation, responsive behavior, and visual hierarchy.
- Motion design, animations, transitions, micro-interactions, timing, and easing.
- Interaction presentation and the overall user experience.

Do not merely imitate Golden's design language or recreate approximately similar components.

Use Golden's actual implementation, reusable structures, styles, assets, and motion patterns wherever authorized and technically appropriate.

The complete Golden reference is located at:

C:\DesignLab\MultipleCorp-Golden-Reference

The frozen Golden lab must remain strictly read-only.

DESIGN REFINEMENT REQUIREMENT

Where Golden's original design can be improved without losing its identity, I want MULTIPLECORP to be:

1. Cleaner and more visually polished.
2. More symmetrical and consistently aligned.
3. Better proportioned across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
4. Comfortable to read and interact with.
5. Visually balanced, with intentional whitespace and sufficient breathing room.
6. Consistent in spacing, density, component sizing, and visual rhythm.
7. Elegant and professional rather than oversized, cramped, excessively sparse, or visually heavy.

Breathing room is important, but it must be intentional. Avoid both excessive empty space and overly dense layouts.

Do not interpret this directive as permission to arbitrarily enlarge or shrink every component.

FIRST REPRODUCE, THEN REFINE

For each authorized page and interaction:

A. Establish the original Golden reference and reproduce its visual and motion behavior as faithfully as possible.

B. Identify differences required by MULTIPLECORP's approved business workflows, data, security, and accessibility.

C. Adapt Golden's design intelligently for those required differences while preserving its visual quality and interaction characteristics.

D. Propose optional visual refinements only where justified. Clearly distinguish these refinements from fidelity corrections, and request my approval for material design deviations.

Do not silently redesign Golden or compromise canonical MULTIPLECORP requirements.

OWNER ACCEPTANCE

Automated tests, screenshot similarity, motion checks, and governance gates are necessary evidence, but they do not constitute my visual approval.

I will evaluate the actual appearance and interaction quality.

Acceptance requires convincing Golden fidelity, polished responsive layouts, consistent proportions, comfortable spacing, correct motion, and satisfactory behavior throughout the relevant user journeys.

The previous GF-1 pilot is NOT visually approved.

EXECUTION CONFIGURATION

I intend to use:

Planning: Claude Opus 5.5, Max.
Executor: Claude Opus 5.5, Ultracode.

Please assess how Ultracode should be authorized and constrained for the corrective pilot, including its workflow, agent coordination, repository protections, verification boundaries, and resource efficiency.

The previous GF-1 authorization's restrictions must not be silently bypassed. Any necessary change to the execution workflow must be explicitly covered by a new authorization.

PLANNING TASK

Prepare a focused corrective plan for the existing four-page Golden-first pilot.

Address the specific visual failures identified during my review, including PS-32, PS-33, PS-35, PS-36, as well as Login, Dashboard, Create Purchase, desktop consistency, responsive behavior, and motion.

Include remediation of GR-02 and the unresolved GR-16 issue.

Establish measurable fidelity checks and explicit Owner visual acceptance checkpoints.

Do not expand to the remaining 53 screens or S2-3 until I have approved the corrected pilot.

Do not authorize implementation immediately. First present your corrective approach, any conflicts requiring my decisions, and a proposed execution strategy optimized for Opus 5.5 Ultracode.
