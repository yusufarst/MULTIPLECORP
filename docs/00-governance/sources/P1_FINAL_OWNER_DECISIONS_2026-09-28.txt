GAP-004 — FINANCIAL SEMANTICS
==================================================

Approved financial semantics:

MultipleCorp must distinguish:

1. Sales / Transaction Value
2. Active Receivable
3. Cash-In
4. Cost / HPP
5. Cash-Out

These concepts must not be collapsed into one financial state.

ISSUED / FINAL INVOICE:
- records the agreed sales/transaction value
- does not imply payment has been received
- does not automatically imply cash-in

BILLED / DITAGIHKAN:
- once an issued invoice is explicitly marked as billed,
  the unpaid amount becomes an active receivable
- record billed_at and due_date
- receivable aging follows the finalized billing/due-date semantics

An issued invoice that has not yet been billed must remain separately
identifiable as:

BELUM DITAGIHKAN

PAYMENT:
- represents actual money received
- reduces the receivable
- creates Cash-In when recorded
- supports partial payment
- supports full payment
- supports overpayment handling
- supports audited correction/reallocation

COST / HPP:
- represents actual/direct economic cost attributed to the project
- is used for managerial profitability

CASH-OUT:
- represents actual money disbursed
- creating a purchase does not automatically mean money has already
  left the business

Do not expand this into full formal accounting.

V1 remains managerial finance.

GAP-006 — CROSS-COMPANY VISIBILITY
==================================================

Use DEFAULT DENY.

SUPER ADMIN / OWNER:
- may access all companies
- may view consolidated information
- may view company-specific information
- may access all financial and inventory attribution information

ADMIN OPERASIONAL:
- may access only explicitly granted companies
- access remains permission/capability based
- company scope must always be enforced server-side

Because MultipleCorp uses one shared physical warehouse:

an Admin with the required inventory permission may view the physical
stock availability necessary to perform warehouse operations.

However, this does NOT automatically grant access to another company's:

- purchase cost
- profitability
- bank accounts
- invoices
- billing
- payments
- financial records
- private documents
- unrelated transaction details

Explicitly separate:

PHYSICAL INVENTORY VISIBILITY

from:

COMPANY BUSINESS / FINANCIAL VISIBILITY

Manual manipulation of URL, IDs, query parameters, request payloads,
API calls, or file-download paths must never bypass company access.

GAP-023 — STOCK RESERVATION AND USABLE STOCK
==================================================

A quotation alone does NOT reserve inventory.

Stock may be reserved only after the commercial requirement has become
confirmed, normally after quotation/order approval.

Conceptually distinguish:

ON HAND
RESERVED
UNUSABLE / QUARANTINED
AVAILABLE

Use the invariant:

AVAILABLE
=
ON HAND
-
RESERVED
-
UNUSABLE

subject to refinement during domain/database design without changing
the business meaning.

Damaged, rejected, or quarantined goods must not count as normally
usable available stock.

A reservation:

- associates quantity with a confirmed project/order
- does not itself create a physical stock-out movement
- must be released or adjusted when the project is cancelled,
  quantity changes, or the reservation is no longer required

Physical stock decreases only when an actual dispatch or other
legitimate physical stock movement occurs.

Concurrency protection must ensure that two simultaneous projects
cannot both successfully reserve or dispatch the final available stock.

Keep the V1 reservation model simple.

Do not build an enterprise allocation engine.
