==================================================
D-1 — TAX TREATMENT
==================================================

DECISION:

Use the NET model, with configurable tax treatment.

Business semantics:

1. Output tax is not part of operating revenue.

2. Purchase tax enters inventory/project cost only when, for the relevant
   company and transaction, it is legitimately non-creditable or otherwise
   part of actual economic acquisition cost.

3. Tax treatment must remain configurable by company, transaction context,
   effective period/date, and supporting evidence.

4. Do not hardcode one universal tax treatment or rate.

5. When a client, government treasurer, SIPLAH operator, or other legitimate
   intermediary withholds or collects tax from an invoice payment and valid
   evidence exists:

   - the evidenced tax amount may settle the corresponding portion of the
     receivable;
   - it creates no Cash-In for that portion;
   - it is not a payment;
   - it is not a write-off;
   - it is not operating revenue;
   - it is not automatically an expense;
   - gross transaction/invoice value remains historically intact;
   - actor, amount, tax type, evidence, date and audit linkage are preserved.

6. Never invent tax formulas.

7. Exact tax computation/configuration belongs to later technical design;
   P3 owns only the business workflow semantics.

==================================================
D-2 — SHIPPING AND OTHER PURCHASE CHARGES
==================================================

DECISION:

Use DIRECT ATTRIBUTION / HYBRID treatment.

1. A purchase charge that is directly attributable to acquiring goods and
   bringing them to their usable/available condition or destination forms
   part of actual acquisition cost.

2. When such a charge applies to multiple goods lines/lots, allocate it
   proportionally using a deterministic basis appropriate to the charge,
   with conservation and existing rounding rules preserved.

3. The allocation basis must be explicit and auditable.

4. A charge that is not directly attributable to inventory acquisition is
   recorded as an expense instead.

5. For project-specific expenses, attribute them to the project where the
   causal relationship is known.

6. For replenishment/general-company expenses, attribute them to the
   relevant company.

7. Never count the same charge both in lot/project cost and expense.

Do not turn this P3 decision into database-schema or accounting-GL design.

==================================================
D-3 — INVENTORY LOSS / DAMAGED RETURN
==================================================

DECISION:

RECORD THE LOSS.

1. Disposal, shrinkage, loss in transit, and client-damaged returns must not
   make economic cost disappear.

2. Record the economic loss exactly once using the relevant actual cost /
   attributed lot cost.

3. Charge the loss to the project when the causal project is known.

4. Otherwise charge it to the economic/owning company.

5. Preserve quantity movement, provenance, reason, evidence where available,
   actor, timestamp and audit history.

6. Never double-count the cost as both HPP and loss expense unless the
   underlying business facts legitimately represent distinct amounts.

D-4 — KUITANSI BEFORE PAYMENT
==================================================

DECISION:

ALLOW a pre-payment Kuitansi for legitimate payment-processing /
administrative purposes.

This is required because institutional/government administrative processes
may require a signed Kuitansi before funds are transferred.

Define clearly:

KUITANSI UNTUK PROSES PEMBAYARAN

may be issued before actual payment when legitimately required.

However:

- it does NOT create Cash-In;
- it does NOT create a Payment fact;
- it does NOT settle the receivable;
- it does NOT mark the invoice paid;
- it must remain distinguishable from evidence that money was actually
  received;
- when actual payment later occurs, the actual Payment fact is recorded and
  linked appropriately;
- the original issued Kuitansi remains immutable under the normal document
  revision/void rules;
- duplicate financial recognition is forbidden.

Update previously approved BR-DOC-04 / DOC-05 semantics only as narrowly
necessary to support this Owner decision.

Preserve historical provenance when amending an approved P2 rule.
