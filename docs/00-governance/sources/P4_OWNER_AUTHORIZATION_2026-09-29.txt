3. OWNER CLARIFICATION — UNATTRIBUTED CROSS-COMPANY LOSS
==================================================

This supersedes only the P3 judgement call that temporarily blocked
unrelated physical dispatch while an unexplained fungible loss awaited
Owner attribution.

OWNER DECISION:

Economic attribution ambiguity must NOT unnecessarily freeze valid physical
warehouse operations.

When an unexplained fungible loss occurs and provenance cannot establish
which legal company economically owns the missing quantity:

1. Correct physical stock truth immediately.

2. Create an UNATTRIBUTED ECONOMIC LOSS CASE.

3. At loss recognition, preserve an immutable attribution snapshot
   containing at minimum:

   - affected product
   - quantity
   - location / relevant scope
   - candidate companies
   - candidate lot/provenance context
   - each candidate company's relevant exposure at recognition
   - business date
   - recorded_at
   - actor
   - reason
   - evidence

4. Do NOT automatically assign the loss using:

   - FIFO
   - oldest-first
   - pro-rata
   - whichever stock moves next
   - another automatic company-selection rule

5. Only Owner resolves the economic/company attribution.

6. Until Owner resolves it:

   - company-specific loss attribution remains pending;
   - no company-level loss/profit effect is fabricated;
   - reports requiring resolved company attribution must expose the pending
     amount clearly;
   - affected completion may remain blocked where the unresolved attribution
     is materially required;
   - Owner action queues must expose the pending case.

7. HOWEVER:

   remaining valid physical stock is NOT reserved/frozen merely to preserve
   future attribution possibilities.

   Normal receiving, reservation, dispatch and fulfillment may continue
   against actual physical stock, subject to the normal inventory invariants.

8. Later physical movement does not alter the historical candidate/exposure
   snapshot captured when the loss was recognized.

9. Owner attribution later assigns the economic loss against that historical
   case; it does not rewrite subsequent stock movements.

10. If later evidence definitively identifies ownership before Owner
    resolution, the case may resolve from that evidence according to the
    normal authority/audit rules.

11. If lost stock is physically found, reverse the physical loss and resolve
    the pending economic case consistently without double recognition.

This is an explicit Owner clarification.

Apply the narrow required P3 amendment and record provenance.
