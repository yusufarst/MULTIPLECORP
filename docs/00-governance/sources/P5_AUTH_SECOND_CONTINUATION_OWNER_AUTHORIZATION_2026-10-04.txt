3. ROUND-2 DISPOSITIONS
==================================================

Every other round-2 finding is Level 1. Resolve each as stated. Where this
section is silent, follow the reviewer's recommended fix.

R2-01  An account becomes high-risk only while active (K1). The activation
       command (NX-02) runs AU-18's test. When activation makes an account
       without confirmed TOTP high-risk, it does exactly what R-05 requires
       of a grant: it makes the password unusable, revokes unused credential
       tokens, ends sessions and issues one RESET link. Role and grant
       commands on an inactive account change nothing until activation.
       Update AU-18, D-SEC-18, AU-12, OD-01, RV-11 (or RV-05), M-45, H8-17
       and the §8 count paragraph.

R2-02  The second-factor limit must survive a store restart, an eviction and
       a failover, and must fail closed when its durable source is
       unavailable. Choose one approach and record why:
       - enforcement that combines the limiter store with a durable floor
         derived from security events, with the indexes this needs; or
       - this one limit kept on a database-backed limiter.
       In either case, state whether the limiter store is in the path and,
       if it is, that its keys are excluded from eviction and that no
       failover store is used for it. Update AU-05, H6-07, H6-16, WS-12,
       D-CC-10, HO-24, RV-12, M-44, H8-17, the index register, and one note
       at SZ-09. Add tests for restart, eviction and failover as P8
       obligations.

R2-03  The incident command runs this protocol:
       a. an opening sweep deletes every session and revokes every unused
          credential token;
       b. while it runs, sign-in, credential-link issue and use, and NX-02
          commands are refused, through a mechanism that needs no new table
          or field (for example the framework's maintenance mode or an
          incident flag);
       c. every account is processed, including any created after the run
          started;
       d. a closing sweep revokes unused tokens and deletes sessions again.
       The leaked key is not listed at all unless P9's inventory finds
       ciphertext that must survive. In H9-14, before issuing RESET links,
       the Owner reviews accounts, grants, credential links and Google links
       created since the suspected leak, and unlinks suspect Google links
       through the existing OWNER unlink. Update AU-21, SX-02, RV-17, H6-15,
       H8-21, H9-14 and HO-41.

R2-05  RV-14, and every non-challenge step of RV-12, re-read under LK-03 the
       password hash named by the session's digest (as RV-10 does) and
       refuse on a difference. Add the race to M-45 and HO-41.

R2-06  Align AU-22 with DIR-043 §7.7 and M-43. A Google callback that
