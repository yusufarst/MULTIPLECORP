CURRENT BUSINESS DECISIONS
==================================================

1. ROLES

Launch includes two built-in roles:

- Super Admin / Owner
- Admin Operasional

However, backend authorization must be permission-based.

In the future, the Owner may create custom roles with selected capabilities.

Do not hard-code business behavior around role names only.

2. CLIENTS

UNY and UGM are normal clients.

Use a generic model:

Organization
→ Unit / Department
→ PIC

Do NOT create:

- UNY Module
- UGM Module
- RT UNY Module

3. SIPLAH

SIPLAH is a Transaction Channel.

It is NOT a separate project system.

V1 does not require direct SIPLAH API integration.

Architecture should remain ready for future integration.

4. SPJ / ADMINISTRATIVE DOCUMENTS

SPJ requirements depend on each client.

Do NOT create a rigid universal SPJ workflow.

V1 should use flexible administrative document requirements/checklists.

Do not build a complex rules engine.

5. SUPPLIER COMPARISON

Supplier Comparison is REMOVED from V1.

Do not reintroduce it.

Keep only:

- Supplier Master
- purchase history
- previous purchase price
- last purchase date
- product-supplier relationships derived from history

6. PURCHASE ORDER

Purchase Order is optional.

Purchases may proceed without a PO.

7. FINANCE

V1 provides managerial finance.

Do NOT implement full formal accounting.

Do not build:

- General Ledger
- complex Chart of Accounts
- full debit/credit journal engine
- balance sheet
- depreciation
- accounting period closing

8. PROJECT

Project is the operational center.

Normal case:

1 Project → 1 primary Company

Do not complicate the normal architecture because of rare cross-company cases.

9. WAREHOUSE

There is one shared physical warehouse.

Physical stock is one pool.

However, receiving and stock movements must remain traceable to the relevant source/cost-owning company where needed.

If stock purchased by Company A is later used by Company B:
the attribution must not silently change.

Design the simplest explicit and auditable V1 solution.

Do not build an enterprise WMS.

==================================================
COST VS CASH-OUT
==================================================

Cost/HPP and Cash-Out are different concepts.

Cost/HPP:
used to calculate actual/direct project cost and profitability.

Cash-Out:
recorded when money is actually paid or disbursed.

Do not assume that creating a purchase automatically means cash has already left the company.

Do not expand this into full accounting.
