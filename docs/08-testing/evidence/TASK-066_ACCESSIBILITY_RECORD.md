# TASK-066 accessibility record

Status: REVIEW | Updated: 2026-11-15 | Owner: Planning

*Written by the executor of [TASK-066](../../11-tasks/TASK-066.md) under [APPR-039](../../00-governance/DECISION_LOG.md#dir-125-and-appr-039--task-066-narrowed-with-its-minimal-fixes-2026-11-15). It records WCAG 2.2 AA's audit of the built pages on the CI stack ([TEST_STRATEGY §11](../TEST_STRATEGY.md#11-accessibility); [DESIGN_SYSTEM §15](../../07-ux-design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#15-accessibility)), the defects it found and their minimal fixes, the keyboard-only runs, the screen-reader scripts prepared for Layer 3 and what stays recorded for the Owner's decision. It is evidence, not a specification: where it and a source differ, the source wins.*

## 1. How the audit ran

- **The engine on every page state** (`tests/Browser/a11y-gate.ts`, called by `auditPage` in `tests/Browser/localization-audit.ts`): axe-core's WCAG 2.2 AA rules (`wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa`) on each state of the 65 pages the localization audit visits — dialogs, sheets, menus and deferred groups included — at the desktop width and at 390 px, on Chromium and WebKit; a violation fails the run (criterion 1). Each reading is kept in `storage/test-results/browser/a11y/<engine>/<state>-<width>.json`, in the localization layer's `browser-evidence-localization` artifact.
- **The measured checks**, once per page and width on Chromium: the keyboard's walk by Tab from the page's top or from an open overlay's — each focused element's ring (the 2 px outline, or a halo the focus adds, on the control or its group), never wholly under a fixed or sticky region (2.4.11), no trap and no positive tab index (2.1.2) —; the same walk under forced colours, each control whose ring is lost; declared movement under reduced motion (D-DS-17); text fields' and controls' boundaries at 3:1 (1.4.11); one `h1`, the skip link, live regions, each status a text.
- **The other measured checks, from TASK-065's gate on the same visits** ([gate record](TASK-065_UI_GATE_RECORD.md)): reflow at 320 px and every band edge without horizontal page scrolling, 1.4.12's text spacing losing no mandated copy, a coarse pointer's 44 px targets.
- **Keyboard-only** (`tests/Browser/Accessibility/keyboard.spec.ts`): the interaction patterns the critical journeys are made of; UXS-41's quotation (CJ-01) and the scans (CJ-07) as they run; every page's walk above (APPR-039).
- **Announcements** (`tests/Browser/Accessibility/announcements.spec.ts`): each outcome in the live region its kind takes.
- **The module specifications** of `tests/Browser/Accessibility/` keep their own WCAG 2.2 AA checks per page at 390 and 1,280 px (each UI Task's DOD-17).

## 2. The runs

- **The measurement** — CI run 43696518553 on `a2bf305`: axe-core found one AA violation — PG-006's figures table at 390 px a scrolling region the keyboard could not reach (`scrollable-region-focusable`, 2.1.1) —, and the focus walk found the keyboard's ring missing on 131 of the 136 page-widths it walked — the controls of 25 class strings, the tabs, the tab panel and the toggle.
- **The first fixes** — CI run 43701092011 on `ba4897c`: the localization layer's audit PASS on every state, no AA violation on either engine; the component gallery's specimen of the system page carried a second `h1` and the dialog's keyboard loop was read before its confirming button was enabled — both corrected in `7585efe`.
- **The second fixes** — CI run 43705483824 on `7585efe`: the localization, accessibility and responsive layers and Verify PASS, no AA violation in 334 state readings; the focus walk's last readings were a page's own initial focus taken for the rest state, three controls behind the phone's docked scan field and a data table's row link measured without its row — the dock fixed, the floor task's 48 px kept on the phone's scan and search fields, and the readings corrected in this record's head, proven by its own fresh CI before the merge (DIR-103). The e2e layer's WebKit half had two waits past 5 s on a navigation still in flight — `tests/Browser/E2E/handover.spec.ts:33` and `numbering.spec.ts:64`, as on pull request #90 — and the performance job stopped at 100 of TL-15's 103 tests (TASK-065's GF-14): both open risks under DIR-124, recorded here, not classified away; the two jobs rerun on the same head passed (attempt 2), every job of run 43705483824 PASS.

## 6. Recorded for the Owner's decision

- **The sign-in pages' frame** — PG-001–PG-004 carry no skip link and no live region at their load: they are one form each, with no repeated navigation to bypass (2.4.1 not applicable), and a field's error is tied to it through `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`, a failed Google sign-in in an alert role; whether a reader announces a field's error as it appears is SR-01's to hear. Recorded N/A with this reason, not judged a defect.
- **A modal's hidden focus guard as a resting stop** — when a dialog's last control is disabled (IP-08's confirmation before its reason is given), one Tab rests on Base UI's hidden focus guard before the next returns into the dialog: focus never reaches the page behind the dialog, but that one stop is unseen. A behaviour of the dialog library, recorded with the walk's count (`guardStops`) rather than changed here.
- **TASK-065's findings** stay as their gate record states them ([§7](TASK-065_UI_GATE_RECORD.md#7-the-findings-for-the-owners-decision)): GF-01 (the composer's tolerance) for resolution before production approval (DIR-124); GF-04–GF-13 for the Owner's decision; GF-02 and GF-03 fixed here.
- **The open risks of DIR-124** — TL-15's performance job reaching its 30-minute limit (GF-14), now in three runs of pull request #90 and #91 together, and a WebKit navigation or save waiting past 5 s in the e2e layer's second half, four times across the two pull requests — `handover.spec.ts` and `numbering.spec.ts` each twice —, each passing on its next run: their cause unconfirmed, both possibly one database slowing as the layer's data grows; recorded for a targeted remediation, never marked fixed.
