## P2 non-relaxation check

WORKFLOWS adds no stored lifecycle state and no lifecycle transition, and relaxes no precondition. Specific checks: consumed reservations are never reactivated (L-07); quotations are never voided; void never cascades; cancellation follows BR-PRJ-01 literally (L-10) and no CANCELLED → ACTIVE path is introduced (L-11); the approved correction legality matrix is applied as written, with decision corrections using COMPENSATING ACTION (L-32) and invoice dispositions required by the existing conservation rules BR-FIN-03/04. Additional preconditions added by P3 enforce existing invariants or Owner decisions only: per-line conservation, restoration caps, anchored settlements, refund-application finality (BR-FIN-03/05), allocation-follows-consumption (BR-INV-05) and the pending-case retention of SF-UNATTRIBUTED. The retention is the one precondition that can reject a dispatch previously valid on AVAILABLE alone; its business impact was reviewed under [change control](../../00-governance/CHANGE_CONTROL.md#decision-authority): it blocks at most the pending quantity times one less than the number of candidate companies, only in the affected scope and only until the Owner resolves the visible case (QS-22), and the alternatives would either let an operator's lot choice silently decide the bearing company (forbidden by DIR-026 items 1, 2 and 6) or force re-pointing of later consumptions across projects. It is therefore the minimal Level-1 safeguard enforcing the Owner's decision (L-42). The only changes to approved P2 text are the Owner-decided `DIR-024`/`DIR-026` amendments and the `TECH-016` Level-1 clarifications listed in DECISION_LOG.
