## 10. Journeys

One journey per workflow of WORKFLOWS §5, named after it. Each row gives the screens and entry points (SCR, [INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE §8](../INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#8-screen-inventory)), the commands with their capability and indivisible action, the branches and refusals, the correction entry points (section 11) and the differences by capability and company. Every command follows section 5: its answers (IP-01), its date (IP-07), its confirmation (IP-08, IP-09) and its identifiers (IP-02) are not repeated per journey. What holds for every journey:

- **Owner and Admin.** The Owner holds every capability and every company (RG-02, RG-03). An Admin sees a screen, a part of a screen or a control only when the capability and the record's company allow it; a missing view capability removes the fields of its family — they are absent, never masked, greyed or shown as zero ([INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE §7](../INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#7-company-context-model)).
- **One company or several.** With one company in scope there is no company filter and no company label on rows; with several, every row carries its company and nothing is totalled across companies for an Admin (CS-08).
- **Other companies.** A record of a company outside scope does not exist for the account (AZ-11); in the pooled warehouse views it appears only as the relation marker (PJ-04). An effect chosen on such a record is refused and routed (IP-06).
- **Continuation.** Progress is derived from facts (WORKFLOWS §1); any authorized account continues open work, and a stale page meets IP-03 (PX-07).

**Pending cases and count surpluses (SCR-27).** SF-UNATTRIBUTED and AX-07 are resolved by these commands, each with its own answer (IP-19):

- **"Tetapkan Perusahaan"** — the Owner's attribution (`stock.resolve_unattributed`; AX-37). The case's recognition snapshot is shown: each candidate company with its exposure — its candidate lots, their remainders in the scope and their net costs at recognition. The Owner assigns quantities to companies, each within that company's exposure and the total within the pending quantity (C-37). For each assigned company the form proposes that company's own lots that still hold claims in the scope, each to be confirmed — oldest first only as a picking aid; a shortfall, because later movements consumed them, is closed only from other lots the Owner chooses one by one, and each such line states that an inter-company allocation charges the assigned company and needs "Alasan alokasi antar-perusahaan" (WF-INV-07). A condition-change case offers only usable claims the assigned company still holds; otherwise the form says the case stays pending. The answer names the loss recorded once, on the causal project or else the company (SF-LOSS).
- **"Tetapkan dengan Bukti"** — an Admin's resolution by definitive later evidence (`stock.resolve_unattributed_evidence`; OD-10). The pool evidence recorded after the case's recognition is linked first as the primary proof (IP-16). The form then offers only candidate lots of the snapshot whose company is in the Admin's scope, with their quantities and without costs, never the number or identity of other candidate companies (PJ-07); each resolved unit is named by lot with its quantity, and the assigned company is shown as derived from the named lots — never chosen: there is no company selector and no shortfall control. Named lots of two companies, a lot that no longer holds its named quantity, or evidence that does not qualify is REJECTED with the rule in words. The reason is mandatory, and the resolution appears in the Owner review queue (QS-14).
- **"Tetapkan Surplus"** — the Owner's attribution of a count surplus (`stock.attribute_surplus`; AX-07). The open finding with its scope, product and quantity; the pending loss cases of the scope it will find first and the identified losses it will reverse are listed before confirming; the company and the cost basis are entered, and the answer says that the basis is flagged (QS-18).
- **"Balikkan Penetapan"** — the exact reversal of a resolution, once (CM-27): an Owner resolution only by the Owner (OD-09); an evidence resolution by an account holding `stock.resolve_unattributed_evidence` or by the Owner (OD-10). The case is pending again with its snapshot unchanged.
