## 1. Scope

- **In**, exactly [SD-05](../../../00-governance/DECISION_LOG.md#dir-061-dir-062-obs-023-and-tech-031--p7-ux-re-baseline-stage-2-decisions-s2-0-authorization-baseline-and-prototype-setup)'s scope: one payment fact — date, an amount above zero, method, the company's bank account, the statement line it matches, reference and proof — with same-command applications ([AX-18](../../../03-workflows/WORKFLOWS/s08-09-corrections-indivisible.md#9-indivisible-business-actions), through SF-APPLY) to issued invoices of the same client and company ([BR-FIN-03](../../../02-domain/BUSINESS_RULES.md#finance-fin); BR-FIN-07); each application at most the invoice's outstanding amount (BR-FIN-04) and their sum at most the payment (BR-FIN-03), the rest staying *Kredit Pelanggan* (BR-FIN-05); the numeric annex's item 1 — an invoice of Rp1.000.000, Rp300.000 paid, Rp700.000 outstanding; the simulated rejections of an application above the outstanding amount and of applications above the payment; a missing proof needing a reason and the payment flagged (L-29).
- **Out**, documented only in §7: fee or tax deductions, refund, reallocation, contra, the Kuitansi link, one statement line for several clients, write-off; a later, separate application is `payment.apply`, outside the journey.
- **Persona:** Dimas, with `payment.record` — which covers same-command applications — and `finance.view` (SP-19). **Viewports:** the desktop at 1440×900 and 1366×768. **Languages:** both.
